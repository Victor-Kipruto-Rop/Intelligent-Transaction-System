# Compliance and Jurisdiction Framework

## Purpose
Provide a framework for assessing obligations; this document does not certify compliance or provide legal advice.

## Assessment dimensions
Operating entity and countries; service role; funds custody or payment initiation; data controller/processor roles; cardholder-data scope; AML/CFT exposure; outsourcing; consumer protection; record retention; cross-border transfers; incident reporting; licensing and local partner requirements.

## Reference frameworks to assess where applicable
GDPR and local privacy laws; PCI DSS if cardholder-data scope applies; ISO 27001/22301; SOC 2 assurance; FATF recommendations and local AML/CFT rules; EU payment services rules where applicable; ISO 20022 message standards; local central-bank and payment-system rules.

## Required process
Maintain a jurisdiction register, legal owner, applicability assessment, control mapping, evidence, review date and change triggers. Obtain qualified legal/compliance review before entering a market or offering regulated capabilities.

## Claims policy
Do not claim certification, licensing, regulatory approval or compliance based solely on architecture documents or tool configuration.
