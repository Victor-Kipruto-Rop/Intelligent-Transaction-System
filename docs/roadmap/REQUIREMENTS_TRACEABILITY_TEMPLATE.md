## Rules

- IDs are stable and never reused.
- Requirements must be testable and have an accountable owner.
- Mark unverified evidence as pending, not passed.
- Record scope exclusions and assumptions.
- Link implementation and test evidence only after they exist.
- A release gate cannot be approved while a critical requirement has no owner or evidence.
