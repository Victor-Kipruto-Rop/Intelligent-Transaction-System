# Global Provider Roadmap

Connector maturity: L0 documented target; L1 adapter skeleton; L2 sandbox contract-tested; L3 production credentials/provider approval; L4 monitored production connector with reconciliation and incident runbooks.

Waves:
1. Generic REST/webhook/file ingestion and internal ledger adapter.
2. M-Pesa and selected local mobile-money/bank integrations based on approved access.
3. Airtel Money, MTN MoMo and additional regional providers.
4. Open Banking and bank statement formats.
5. Global payment processors and card settlement feeds.
6. ERP/accounting platforms and enterprise CDC.
7. Additional regional and specialized financial systems.

Provider names are roadmap targets, not claims of partnership, API access, certification or implementation. Each connector needs legal/provider approval, sandbox fixtures, contract tests, security review, operational support and reconciliation evidence.
